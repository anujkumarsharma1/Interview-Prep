# Amazon Leadership Principles

Amazon (and many other companies) use these principles to evaluate candidates.
Each answer should follow the [STAR method](./star-method.md).

## The 16 Principles

| # | Principle | Core Idea |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Customer Obsession | Start with the customer and work backwards |
| 2 | Ownership | Act on behalf of the entire company, not just your team |
| 3 | Invent and Simplify | Expect innovation; find ways to simplify |
| 4 | Are Right, A Lot | Have strong judgment; seek diverse perspectives |
| 5 | Learn and Be Curious | Always seek to improve and explore new ideas |
| 6 | Hire and Develop the Best | Raise the performance bar; coach others |
| 7 | Insist on the Highest Standards | Drive quality; fix problems at the root |
| 8 | Think Big | Create bold direction that inspires results |
| 9 | Bias for Action | Speed matters; take calculated risks |
| 10 | Frugality | Accomplish more with less |
| 11 | Earn Trust | Listen, speak candidly, be self-critical |
| 12 | Dive Deep | Stay connected to details; be skeptical of metrics alone |
| 13 | Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit | Challenge respectfully; then commit fully |
| 14 | Deliver Results | Focus on key inputs; deliver with the right quality |
| 15 | Strive to Be Earth's Best Employer | Create a safe, productive, diverse environment |
| 16 | Success and Scale Bring Broad Responsibility | Be humble about impact; do better for the world |

## Frequently Tested Principles

| Principle | Common Question Type |
|---|---|
| Customer Obsession | Tell me about a time you went above and beyond for a customer/user |
| Ownership | Tell me about a time you took ownership outside your scope |
| Invent and Simplify | Tell me about a time you simplified a complex process |
| Bias for Action | Tell me about a time you had to make a decision with limited data |
| Dive Deep | Tell me about a time you used data to solve a problem |
| Have Backbone; Disagree and Commit | Tell me about a time you disagreed with your team/manager |
| Deliver Results | Tell me about your most impactful project |
| Earn Trust | Tell me about a time you gave difficult feedback |

## Preparation Tips

1. Map each story in your bank to **2–3 principles** it demonstrates.
2. For each of the top 7 principles above, have at least one dedicated story ready.
3. When answering, explicitly name the principle behavior you're demonstrating (e.g., "I took ownership of the issue even though it wasn't my team's responsibility…").
