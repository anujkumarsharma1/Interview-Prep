# Common Behavioral Questions

Use the [STAR method](./star-method.md) to structure your answers.

## Self / Background

- Tell me about yourself.
- Walk me through your resume.
- Why do you want to work here?
- Where do you see yourself in 5 years?

## Problem Solving & Technical

- Tell me about a technically challenging project you worked on.
- Describe a time you had to learn a new technology quickly.
- Tell me about a time you debugged a difficult problem.
- Describe a time you made a technical decision with incomplete information.

## Teamwork & Collaboration

- Tell me about a time you worked with a difficult team member.
- Describe a situation where you had to collaborate across teams.
- Tell me about a time you gave or received critical feedback.
- Describe a time you mentored or helped a colleague.

## Leadership & Ownership

- Tell me about a time you led a project.
- Describe a time you took ownership of something beyond your role.
- Tell me about a time you influenced a decision without formal authority.
- Describe a time you had to make an unpopular decision.

## Failure & Learning

- Tell me about a time you failed.
- Describe a mistake you made and what you learned.
- Tell me about a time a project didn't go as planned.

## Conflict & Trade-offs

- Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager.
- Describe a time you had to prioritize competing tasks.
- Tell me about a trade-off you had to make under pressure.

## Questions to Ask the Interviewer

- What does a typical day look like for this role?
- What does success look like in the first 6 months?
- What are the biggest technical challenges the team is facing?
- How does the team approach code reviews and engineering standards?
- What's the team's culture around work-life balance?
